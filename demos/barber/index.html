About

A barbershop that runs on time.

Greythorn Lane Barbers opened in 2019 when Sam and Theo decided Balwyn North needed a barbershop that ran on time. We take bookings by phone but keep two chairs open for walk-ins, because a haircut shouldn't need a week's notice.

Kids are welcome, pensioners get $5 off on weekday mornings, and if you're not happy with the cut we fix it on the spot.
