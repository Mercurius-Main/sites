About Greythorn Corner

From a window
to a corner cafe.

Greythorn Corner opened in 2019 as a six-seat coffee window and grew into the corner cafe it is today. Everything baked is made here before 6am.

We roast with a small Melbourne roaster and change the single origin every fortnight.
